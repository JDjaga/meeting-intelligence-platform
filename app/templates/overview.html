Paste a meeting. Get the actions.

ProjectActionGraph turns a messy transcript or recording into decisions, action items, owners and deadlines — every item quoted from your transcript and confidence-scored. Vague promises go to a human review queue instead of slipping through.

How it works — 3 steps
1

Give it a meeting

Upload a transcript (.txt/.srt/.vtt) or audio (.mp3/.wav), or load a 1-click demo above. Speech-to-text runs automatically for audio.

2

AI structures it

Explicit promises ("I'll finish Friday") and soft ones ("I'll take a look") become action · owner · deadline · confidence · evidence — never free-form text.

3

Review & track

Low-confidence items wait in the review queue. Open items carry into the next meeting; overdue and analytics live on this site.
